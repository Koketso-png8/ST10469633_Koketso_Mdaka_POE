Our story

We believe every celebration deserves a sweet touch.

Lind's Sweet Treats is a small locally owned business established in early 2025. We specialise in customised candy bouquets, snack hampers and sweet treats.

Our business was created to offer customers creative, affordable and beautifully packaged gifts that make every celebration memorable. Each gift is an opportunity to share joy, show appreciation and make someone feel special.

Our products are created for individuals, families, students, event planners and businesses looking for unique and affordable gift ideas.
